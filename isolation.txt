# AirBnBrrr: AI-Powered HVAC IoT Anomaly Detection System
## How Isolation Forest Works (Plain English Guide)

## 1. What is Isolation Forest?

Imagine you are looking at a crowd of 100 people all wearing plain white t-shirts, and 1 person wearing a bright red suit with a clown hat. 

If you want to spot the odd one out, you don't need to examine all 100 white t-shirts in detail — the person in the red suit immediately stands out because they look completely different from everyone else.

That is exactly how **Isolation Forest** works for your air conditioner:
- Instead of trying to define every single detail of what "perfect" AC operation looks like, it focuses on **isolating things that look weird or unusual**.
- It constantly checks all the sensor readings coming from your AC (power draw, motor temperatures, fan speeds, room humidity, and vibration) to catch odd behavior early.

## 3. Why Do We Use It in AirBnBrrr?

In your air conditioning system, **Isolation Forest is the First Line of Defense (Layer 1)**.

### Why it's helpful:
1. **Catches Hidden & Combined Problems**: 
   Sometimes, a single sensor might look okay on its own. For example:
   - Your voltage might be slightly low (still legal),
   - Your motor temperature might be slightly warm (still under limit),
   - Your compressor vibration might be slightly elevated.
   
   Individually, none of those break a hard safety rule. But **together**, that combination is very abnormal! Isolation Forest notices that this combination has never happened before and flags it.

2. **Prevents Unnecessary False Alarms**:
   It compares new sensor readings against historical data from your AC to decide whether something is genuinely unusual or just normal daily variation.

## 4. How Does It Work Step-by-Step?

You can think of Isolation Forest as playing a game of **"20 Questions"** with your AC sensor data:

### Step 1: Learning Normal Patterns
When the system starts up, it looks at past sensor readings recorded from your AC (power, voltage, temperature, vibration, etc.). It calculates average numbers so it knows what normal operation looks like.

### Step 2: Playing "20 Questions" to Isolate Data
Every time your AC sends new sensor readings (e.g., every few seconds or minutes), the algorithm asks random true/false questions about the readings:
- *"Is the power draw above 600 Watts?"*
- *"Is the compressor temperature above 70 °C?"*
- *"Is the vibration frequency above 80 Hz?"*

### Step 3: Spotting Anomalies
- **Normal Readings (Green Dots)**: Take **many questions** to isolate because they look just like thousands of other normal readings recorded in the past.
- **Anomalous Readings (Red Dots)**: Get isolated in just **1 or 2 questions** because they stand out so clearly.

If a set of sensor readings gets isolated almost instantly, the algorithm says: **"This is an Anomaly!"**

---

## 5. What Happens When an Anomaly is Detected?

When Isolation Forest detects an anomaly, it starts a simple 4-step chain reaction to protect your AC:

1. **Flagged**: The system marks the incoming data reading as suspicious (Red Dot).
2. **Safety Rule Check**: A deterministic rule engine checks exact physical limits (like checking if the power draw exceeds 600W or if the filter is blocked by dust).
3. **AI Expert Explanation**: An AI diagnostic assistant analyzes the readings to write a plain-English explanation of what might be wrong (e.g., *"Compressor Overheating due to Dirty Condenser Coil"*).
4. **Instant Alert**: An alert is created on your AirBnBrrr dashboard, and an SMS notification is sent to your phone so you can take action before your AC breaks down.
